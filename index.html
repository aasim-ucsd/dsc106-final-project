How Hot Will It Get?

Two climate futures, 1850–2100

Muska Mesdaq · Ayesha Asim · Ryan Kasem

A child born today could live through a world that is less than 1 °C warmer than their birth year, or one that is about 4.5 °C warmer. Which one depends on the choices we make about emissions.

This page uses CMIP6 climate model projections to compare two possible futures. Under SSP1-2.6, emissions fall sharply and warming levels off. Under SSP5-8.5, emissions stay very high and warming keeps speeding up. Both futures share the same past. Scroll down to see where they split, and how much that split could matter to you.
